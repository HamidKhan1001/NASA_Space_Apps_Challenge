The data behind the view

Four NASA Earth observation products, chosen because each answers a question farmers ask before planting.

SMAP

Soil moisture

Tells you how wet the root zone is before the canal is opened, so irrigation starts on evidence rather than habit.

Top 5 cm · daily

ECOSTRESS

Crop water stress

Thermal readings show which blocks are working hardest for their water, and which are quietly running dry.

Field scale · 1–4 days

GPM

Rainfall

Rain estimates for the valley and the catchment above it, useful for planning around the weather that is coming.

Global · 3-hourly

Landsat

Land change

Decades of imagery to see how fields, channels, and tree cover have shifted, and to judge what the land can take.

30 m · 16-day revisit

How it is used

Three decisions, one page

The tool is deliberately narrow. It supports the choices that matter most in a single season.

01 / CROP

Pick the next crop

Compare mung bean, maize, and sesame against the moisture and rainfall you expect this season, not the average year.

02 / WATER

Time the irrigation

Watch soil moisture and rainfall together to decide when the canal runs and when it can wait another week.

03 / SOIL

Keep the soil working

Rotate legumes through cereal cycles and track whether the land is holding its condition season after season.
